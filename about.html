About Us

A Legacy of Craft Since 2004.

Inspired by the artisanal gelato traditions of Cremona, we blend authentic Italian mastery with the finest produce from Queensland.

For over two decades, our laboratory has been devoted to the art of handcrafted gelato – authentic artisan Italian-inspired creations. 200+ flavours created in our studio.

Every batch is made slowly and in small quantities, using traditional methods passed down through generations. From selecting the finest seasonal produce to perfecting each recipe by hand, our commitment to quality never wavers — because true gelato can't be rushed.

Effe

The Heart of Our Laboratory.

Our traditional Effe machine, crafted in Italy, sits at the heart of our laboratory. Though slow and time-intensive, it lets us create authentic small-batch gelato with exceptional texture and flavour.

Pozzetti

Our traditional pozzetti preserve gelato at the ideal temperature, protecting its texture and flavour. Unlike open display cabinets, this authentic Italian method keeps every scoop true to its recipe.

Tradition

Authentic Italian Tradition

Rooted in the time-honoured craft of Italy's finest gelaterias, our recipes stay true to the methods passed down through generations. Every flavour is built on authentic technique and quality ingredients, bringing the true taste of Italian gelato-making to every scoop.

From the way we fold our base to the patience we give each batch, every step honours the artisans of Cremona who came before us. It's a tradition of care, not shortcuts — the same slow, hands-on process that has defined true Italian gelato for generations, brought to life fresh in our laboratory every day.

The Art

The Art of Making Gelato

Crafted Slowly. Made with Passion.

Our traditional Effe machine, crafted in Italy, combines slow and time-intensive production with small-batch preparation to create exceptional texture, richness and flavour. Every batch moves through the machine at its own unhurried pace, allowing air to fold gently into the mixture rather than being forced in — the difference between industrial ice cream and true gelato.

This dedication to craft is what the Italians call lentezza, the art of slowness, and it's the same philosophy passed down to us from Cremona's oldest gelaterias. Nothing here is rushed, because nothing worth tasting ever is.
